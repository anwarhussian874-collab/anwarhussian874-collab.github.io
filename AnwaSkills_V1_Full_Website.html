How AnwaSkills works

1. Create your account → 2. Choose a learning package → 3. Study your courses → 4. Track your progress → 5. Use your referral tools when available → 6. Manage your account from the dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AnwaSkills?

AnwaSkills is a learning platform concept where members can access skill-based courses and manage their learning account.

Can I track my learning progress?

Yes. The V1 dashboard includes course progress, completed lessons and certificates.

Where can I see my earnings?

After signing in, open Dashboard → Earnings to view Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days and Total Earnings.

Is payment connected in this V1?

No. Payment integration is intentionally left for a later version.
