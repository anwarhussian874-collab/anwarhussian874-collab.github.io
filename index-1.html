FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I receive after purchasing a package?

Depending on the package, you can receive access to the digital resources included in that package.

Can I access my resources later?

The platform is designed to provide access to purchased digital resources through your account.

How does the referral system work?

Registered users can receive a unique referral link and track eligible referrals through their dashboard. Any rewards or commissions will be subject to the platform's published terms.

Can I use the website on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop computers.
